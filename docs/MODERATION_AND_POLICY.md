# Moderation, Complaints & Policy

## Platform responsibility
Onushondhan is a publishing/hosting/community platform. It does not represent itself as the author, investigator, verifier, or endorser of each user report.

## Investigator responsibility
Every investigator must accept the current Terms of Use, Privacy Policy, Content Policy, and Investigator Responsibility Agreement as applicable before publishing.

Policy text should cover:
- responsibility for claims and evidence;
- rights/permissions for submitted materials;
- defamation/privacy/copyright risk;
- prohibited content;
- malicious files;
- harassment;
- personal data exposure;
- impersonation;
- illegal content;
- abuse of tips/comments/support.

These are platform policy requirements, not legal advice. Legal counsel should review final wording and jurisdiction-specific compliance.

## Moderation actions
- no_action
- request_correction
- warn
- restrict
- hide
- remove
- restore
- lock_comments
- suspend_user
- ban_user
- escalate

## Complaint process
Categories:
- factual dispute
- privacy
- copyright
- personal information
- prohibited content
- harassment
- spam
- other terms violation

A factual dispute should not cause the platform to claim that it has independently verified either side. The workflow should document the complaint, evidence supplied, review action, and outcome.

## Public statuses
When content is withdrawn, restricted, or removed, preserve an auditable internal record. Public-facing wording should be factual and not disclose unnecessary sensitive moderation information.
