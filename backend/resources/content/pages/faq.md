### Does Onushondhan verify investigations?
No. Onushondhan is a publishing platform. Investigators are responsible for their reports. Moderation enforces the Content Policy; it is not fact-checking.

### What does "protected public identity" mean?
An investigator can publish under a public name that is not their legal name. Onushondhan keeps the link to their account and any private details they provide, with tightly restricted and logged access. It is not a guarantee of absolute anonymity.

### Can I send a tip anonymously?
Yes. Signed-in users can hide their identity from the investigator. You can also send a tip as a guest without an account — keep the access code you are given, because it is the only way to read replies.

### What does supporting an investigator mean?
It is a community engagement signal — one per person — and not a statement that their work is accurate.

### How do I report a problem with content?
Use the "Report" option on the investigation, evidence or comment. Choose the category that fits best, including "factual dispute".

### Can photos I upload reveal my location?
Photos can contain GPS metadata. Onushondhan strips metadata from public image renditions, but video, audio, PDF and office files are served as uploaded — remove metadata first.
