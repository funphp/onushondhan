## For readers

Browse the latest and trending investigations, search by keyword, category, tag or location, and read reports with their public evidence and sources. Create an account to comment, bookmark, follow investigators and show support.

## For investigators

1. Register and verify your email address.
2. Create an investigator profile and choose how you appear publicly — under your real name or a protected public identity.
3. Draft your investigation: body, findings, timeline, sources and evidence. Drafts save automatically.
4. Preview it exactly as readers will see it.
5. Confirm your responsibility for the report, then publish. Later changes are published as new, versioned updates.

## Tips

Anyone can send a tip to an investigator — as a signed-in user (optionally hidden from the investigator) or as a guest with no account. Guests receive a one-time access code to read replies.

## Support is not a score

Supporting an investigator is a way to show community appreciation. Support counts and rankings are **not** measures of accuracy or quality.
