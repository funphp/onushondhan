## Purpose

This policy explains what may be published on onusondhan.com and how moderation works. Moderation enforces these rules; **it does not verify the factual accuracy of reports**.

## Prohibited content

- Content that is illegal in the applicable jurisdiction.
- Harassment, threats, intimidation or incitement to violence.
- Hate speech targeting people on the basis of protected characteristics.
- Publication of private personal information (such as home addresses, ID numbers, private phone numbers or medical records) that is not necessary and proportionate to a matter of public interest.
- Sexual content involving minors (reported to authorities) and non-consensual intimate imagery.
- Impersonation of people or organisations.
- Content that infringes copyright or other rights without permission or a lawful basis.
- Malware, malicious files or links, and attempts to compromise the platform or its users.
- Spam, fraud, and manipulation of engagement (support, follows, shares, votes or reactions).

## Investigation standards

Investigators should clearly distinguish documented findings from allegation and opinion, cite sources, describe evidence accurately, avoid presenting unverified claims about named individuals as established fact, and correct errors promptly. These are expectations for investigators; onusondhan.com does not certify compliance.

## Comments

Keep discussion relevant and respectful. Do not post personal information about others. Comments may be hidden automatically when several people report them, pending human review — this is not a finding against the author.

## Complaints

Anyone can file a complaint using the "Report" option. Categories: factual dispute, privacy, copyright, personal information, prohibited content, harassment, spam and other terms violations. For factual disputes we record the dispute and may ask the investigator to review it; we do not adjudicate who is right.

## Moderation actions

Possible outcomes include: no action, a request for correction, a warning, restriction pending review, hiding or removal of content, locking comments, and suspension or blocking of accounts. Moderation records are kept for accountability, and affected users are notified where appropriate.
