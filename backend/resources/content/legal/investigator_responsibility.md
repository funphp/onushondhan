## Investigator Responsibility Agreement

By becoming an investigator on Onushondhan and before each publication you confirm that:

1. **You are responsible for your reports.** You alone are responsible for the claims, statements, findings, evidence, sources and other material you publish, and for the consequences of publishing them.
2. **You have the necessary rights and permissions** for all material you submit, including evidence, images, recordings, documents and quotations, and you have considered the privacy of the people involved.
3. **You understand the legal risks** of publication, including defamation, privacy, data-protection, copyright and other laws that may apply to you, and you will seek independent legal advice where appropriate.
4. **Publication is subject to Onushondhan's policies and applicable law.** Your content may be restricted, hidden or removed under the Content Policy or in response to lawful requests.
5. **Onushondhan does not verify or endorse your report.** Publication on the platform does not mean Onushondhan has independently verified your report or agrees with it, and you will not represent otherwise.
6. **Protected identity is not absolute anonymity.** If you publish under a protected public identity, Onushondhan keeps your account relationship and any private identity details you provide. Access is restricted and logged, but disclosure may be required by law.
7. **You will not upload malicious files** and you will remove sensitive metadata you do not intend to publish.
8. **You will handle tips responsibly,** protect the confidentiality of tipsters, and not attempt to identify anonymous tipsters.
9. **You will correct errors** and respond in good faith to requests for correction and to factual-dispute complaints.

Each confirmation you make at publication is recorded with a timestamp and the version of this agreement in force.
