## 1. Who we are

Onushondhan operates a publishing and community platform for independent investigators. *[Insert legal entity name, registered address and data-protection contact.]*

## 2. What we collect

- **Account data:** name (kept private), public username, email address, password hash, optional phone number, account status and timestamps.
- **Investigator data:** public identity (public name, bio, avatar) and, separately, an optional private identity record (legal or private name and contact details). Private identity data is encrypted at rest, never shown publicly, and accessible only to the investigator and specifically authorised staff, with every staff access logged.
- **Content:** investigations, evidence files, sources, comments, tips and messages you submit.
- **Agreement records:** which version of each agreement you accepted, when, and from which IP address and browser.
- **Security records:** sign-in attempts, security events and an audit trail of high-risk actions.
- **Analytics:** page and engagement events. We do not store raw IP addresses in analytics; visitors are represented by a keyed hash that changes daily.

## 3. Guest tips

If you send a tip without an account we do not create an account, and we do not attach your IP address or browser details to the tip. You receive a one-time access code; we store only a hash of it. Contact details you choose to provide are shared with the investigator you contact.

## 4. How we use data

To operate the platform; to authenticate you and keep accounts secure; to deliver notifications you have chosen; to moderate content and respond to complaints; to prevent abuse and manipulation; to comply with legal obligations; and to understand aggregate usage.

## 5. Evidence files and metadata

Uploaded files can contain hidden metadata (for example GPS location in photos). For public images we serve a re-encoded copy with metadata removed. Other file types (videos, audio, PDFs and office documents) are served as uploaded — **remove sensitive metadata before uploading**.

## 6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data with service providers that host or operate the platform under contract, and where required by law or to protect rights and safety. *[Counsel to list processors and international transfer safeguards.]*

## 7. Retention

- Analytics events: 180 days (daily visitor hashes are removed after 30 days).
- Sign-in logs: 365 days. Security events: 730 days.
- Audit logs, moderation records and agreement acceptances: retained as the platform's forensic record.
- Account and content data: while your account is active and as long as required for legal or safety purposes after deactivation.

## 8. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and to object to processing. Contact us to exercise them. *[Counsel to tailor to applicable law.]*

## 9. Security

We use encryption in transit, encryption of sensitive fields at rest, access controls, audit logging and malware scanning where available. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot promise absolute anonymity.

We will ask you to accept material changes to this policy. Previous versions remain on record.
